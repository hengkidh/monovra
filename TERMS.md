## 1. What the app is

MONOVRA is a free Home launcher for Android car head units and in-car tablets. It shows a clock,
music controls, app shortcuts and, while Google Maps or Waze is navigating, the next turn taken from
that app's notification. It is not an Android Auto or Android Automotive OS app and is not made by,

## 2. Safe use while driving

- Keep your attention on the road. Set up the app, shortcuts and themes only while the vehicle is
  parked.
- Obey traffic laws and road signs at all times. The next-turn display is a convenience copy of
  what your navigation app shows; it can be late, incomplete or wrong. Always follow your
  navigation app, road signs and your own judgement.
- Do not interact with the screen in a way that distracts you or breaks local laws on device use
  while driving.
